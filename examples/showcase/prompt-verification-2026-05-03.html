Workflow Coverage

Formulate

Natural-language brief to constrained blend.

  • Parser normalizes vertical, cost, bio, and performance targets.
  • Controller rejects outputs that miss requested constraints.

EcoMetrics

Sustainability profile from the actual latest blend.

  • Carbon, toxicity, renewability, biodegradability, and regulatory axes.
  • No placeholder recomputation after this fix path.

Regulatory

Vertical-specific screening posture.

  • EU cosmetics, detergents, food, ag, pharma, paint, and industrial frameworks.
  • Shown only when the base blend is valid.

Carbon

Batch-level displacement and circularity economics.

  • Shows CO2e displacement, circularity grade, and credit value estimate.
  • Suppressed for infeasible or constraint-failed blends.

Stability

Early shelf-life, viscosity, pH, packaging, and risk signals.

  • Helps move from formula idea to lab plan.
  • Paired with formulation output rather than isolated assumptions.

Certifications

Certification screening across relevant schemes.

  • Flags likely paths such as USDA BioPreferred, EPA Safer Choice, EU Ecolabel, COSMOS, or ISO 16128 where applicable.
  • Presented as screening, not a substitute for third-party certification.

Pareto

Multi-objective optimization frontier.

  • Compares cost, bio-based content, and performance tradeoffs.
  • Useful when teams need options instead of a single recipe.

QSAR

Molecular property prediction for candidate ingredients.

  • Scores biodegradability, ecotoxicity, performance, and sustainability signals from SMILES.
  • Supports ingredient vetting before optimization.

Reformulation and Memory

Failure diagnosis plus learning from prior runs.

  • Reformulation suggests fixes for viscosity, stability, compliance, and cost failures.
  • Memory records valid formulation events for future pattern insight.

What Changed

Direct regex mode

LLM_PROVIDER=regex now uses the deterministic parser directly, without probing remote providers first.

Cleaner vertical parsing

Body wash maps to personal care, and tablet film coating maps to pharmaceutical before generic coatings.

Solver bound fix

Function bounds no longer force every matching ingredient into the formula as trace filler.

Truthful reporting

Controller output fails closed when a blend misses cost, bio-based, or performance requirements.
